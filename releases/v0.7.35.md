# v0.7.35 — Display-face transport provenance

Oral-B Live now exposes the transport provenance of display-face readings, so advertisement, direct-brush, and iO Sense charger values can be distinguished reliably.

## Changes

- The **Smiley** entity now includes:
  - `smiley_raw`: the unmodified numeric face value
  - `source`: `advertisement`, `direct_brush`, or `charger_bridge`
- The **Last session** entity now includes `display_face_source` alongside `display_face`.
- Display-face provenance is retained across Home Assistant restarts.
- Advertisement, direct-brush, charger-bridge, and restore behavior are covered by automated tests.

## Hardware finding

A controlled Oral-B iO 10 Daily Clean session was stopped at exactly 1:51:

- The physical handle displayed an ordinary blue smile.
- The full advertisement stream reported raw `0` / `off`.
- Without another brushing session, a direct FF0A read returned raw `5` / `special_5`.

A separate 1:59 charger-bridge session also paired raw `5` / `special_5` with the same ordinary smile. The transport source therefore matters when interpreting face values.
