# Charger battery refresh

## Changes

- Refresh brush battery every 30 seconds during charger-managed brushing.
- Read battery immediately when the charger first reports the session idle, before it releases the brush connection.
- Keep pressure sampling at 1 Hz and locally advance the timer during battery ticks.
- Document the charger battery refresh lifecycle and disconnected-brush limitation.

This builds on v0.7.12's toothbrush firmware/device metadata and pacer-label corrections.
