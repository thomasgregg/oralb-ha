# v0.7.34b1 — Idle pressure beta

Beta validation build for #21.

## Changes

- Pressure is unknown while the brush is not actively brushing.
- Advertisement pressure/status byte 4 is decoded as a bit field: bit 7 means high pressure; button bits no longer appear as high.
- Direct and charger FF0B pressure remains low, normal, or high during active brushing.
- Cached advertisements do not restore transient pressure.
- Pressure raw, force, drive diagnostics, and pressure-specific source are cleared outside active brushing.

## Validation requested

1. Leave the handle idle for at least 90 seconds and confirm Pressure remains unknown across reconnect cycles.
2. In Home Assistant direct mode, confirm low, normal, and high pressure during brushing.
3. If using passive advertisements, confirm normal and high pressure during brushing.
4. Confirm mode and power button presses do not appear as high pressure.
5. Confirm completed-session pressure counts remain reasonable.

HACS testers must opt into pre-releases for the Oral-B Live repository or choose v0.7.34b1 under Need a different version, then restart Home Assistant.
