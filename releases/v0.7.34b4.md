# v0.7.34b4 — Signed FF0B force beta

Fourth beta validation build for #26. It includes the earlier v0.7.34 beta fixes and adds corrected FF0B force handling.

- The FF0B force word is decoded as a signed little-endian 16-bit value.
- Negative motor-start settling samples remain visible for live diagnostics but are excluded from local session average and maximum pressure.
- Zero and all positive force measurements remain eligible for the aggregates; no arbitrary upper cutoff is applied.
- Pressure-state events and low/high time tracking continue to include every FF0B sample.
- Regression coverage exercises the observed `0xF830`, `0xFE02`, and `0xFFE9` settling sequence plus genuine positive samples on both direct and charger-forwarded paths.
