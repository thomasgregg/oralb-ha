# v0.7.24 — SmartRing read reliability

## Changes

- Prioritize the toothbrush FF2B SmartRing color read immediately after the post-session battery read.
- Keep the proven battery-first and refill-before-retained-session ordering.
- Improve reliability when the handle is returned to the iO Sense shortly after brushing.

Verified against a real iO Sense/handle pair: charger passthrough returned SmartRing color `#9BFF00` with fourth byte `0`.
