# Stable diagnostic cleanup

This release keeps the protocol-6 battery protection from v0.7.36 while removing the temporary diagnostic surface used to investigate issue #33.

## Changes

- Remove temporary `ff05_raw`, `ff05_source`, `ff29_raw`, `protocol_version`, and `firmware_revision` attributes from the Battery entity.
- Remove successful-read raw FF05/FF29 capture state and debug logging.
- Keep the protocol-aware FF29 dispatcher and protocol-6 fail-safe behavior unchanged.
- Keep the stable Battery attributes limited to `last_read` and `source`.

The v0.7.36b1 and v0.7.36b2 prereleases remain available for the structured protocol-6 research captures. No cloud credentials were exposed by those diagnostics; they contained local brush telemetry only.

Validation: 172 tests, HACS validation, and Hassfest all pass.
