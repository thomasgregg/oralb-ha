# v0.7.34 — Validated pressure, sessions, force, pacer, and charger handling

This is the stable release of the cumulative v0.7.34 beta series. It can be installed through the normal HACS update path; pre-release/beta selection is not required.

## Changes

### Pressure and idle-state correctness

- Pressure is unknown whenever the brush is not actively brushing.
- Advertisement pressure uses the verified status bit field, so genuine high pressure is recognized and button activity is not misreported as pressure.
- Cached advertisements cannot restore stale pressure.
- selection_menu remains provisional until real timer progress confirms brushing.

### Session handling

- Short motor pauses and resumes are merged into one logical session when the authoritative brush timer continues.
- Final duration, sector coverage, pressure summary, and the final display face are retained across resumed fragments.
- Completed sessions finalize once; late retained summaries refine the existing record without increasing sessions_today again.

### FF0B force decoding

- Startup force words are decoded as signed 16-bit values.
- Negative motor-start settling samples remain available diagnostically but no longer inflate average or maximum pressure.

### Pacer hardening

- A valid FF26 schedule is authoritative over conflicting FF09 total hints.
- A successfully read empty FF26 schedule no longer publishes a false eight-sector routine or sector_8 from the final sentinel.
- Genuine configured four-, six-, and eight-sector schedules and the failed-FF26 fallback remain supported.

### iO Sense charger sessions

- Charger pre_run is provisional and does not expose running, pressure, timer, or create a session.
- Native run, active_running, FF04 running, or an advancing FF08 timer confirms the session.
- Quiet states, charger unavailability, and transport disconnects discard provisional ownership and clear confirmed ownership cleanly.

## Validation

- 164 automated tests passed on the supported minimum environment: Home Assistant 2024.4 / Python 3.12.
- The same 164 tests also passed during beta validation on Home Assistant 2026.8 / Python 3.14.
- HACS and Hassfest validation passed.
- Hardware validation passed on an iO Series model 54 / firmware 00.82.26 with an iO Sense charger 0.3.4.
- Issues #21, #22, and #26 were closed after targeted hardware confirmation.
