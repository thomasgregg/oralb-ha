# v0.7.16 — Charger session authority and live updates

## Changes

- Treat the iO Sense brush status as the live-session authority on firmware that leaves session status inactive while brushing.
- Restore charger-forwarded live pressure and mode updates, with safe retries and uninterrupted session tracking.
- Prevent timer-less menu/docking blips and stale advertisements from replacing a real completed session.
- Keep periodic battery refreshes alongside the five-second brush-status check.
- Display brush-head active-use lifetime in hours while retaining the raw seconds attribute.
